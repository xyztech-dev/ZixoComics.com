Power doesn't teach you how to fight, and raw strength doesn't keep people alive. When a sub-basement bio-electrical experiment in Iron City goes catastrophically wrong, Bryan Cole emerges hovering on a column of white-blue arc lightning—reborn as Electromark.

With zero martial arts training and a mind that treats every battle like a circuit board, Bryan must stay anchored to his humanity through his girlfriend Chloe while uniting an impulsive Ares war-god (Chris Thorne / Arion), a fast-talking teenage speedster (Jonah Reed / Shift), a 9-foot obsidian colossus (Dr. James Vance), a shadow kunoichi (Maya Lin / Corvus), and a cursed wolf-panther hybrid (Marcus Bell / Slate).

Team Charter • Directive 01

THE PANTHEON-X PROTOCOL

Forged atop Malachai’s ruined spire in Issue #6, the Pantheon-X Protocol is an off-grid alliance architected by Bryan Cole (Electromark).

  • 01. Tactical Restraint: High-output fighters like Arion are deployed only after exit vectors and civilian containment are secured.
  • 02. Zero Corporate Oversight: Pantheon-X answers to no government directorate or defense contractor.
  • 03. Human Anchors: Every member maintains a civilian life—ensuring gods and monsters never detach from the people they protect.
Architect: Bryan Cole Status: Active
Setting & Human Core

IRON CITY & CHLOE

Chloe — Bryan's Humanity Anchor

Bryan’s civilian girlfriend in Midtown Iron City. She doesn't care about megawatts or hovering—she calls him out whenever he starts speaking like a detached deity instead of the man she loves.

Sub-Level Four & The Relay Station

From the clandestine sub-basement labs beneath Pier 19 to the abandoned Midtown relay station where Bryan engineered Marcus Bell’s suppressor cuff, Iron City’s industrial grid serves as Bryan’s personal weapon.

ROGUES & WARLORDS

Director Sterling Pierce: Corporate black-ops head behind the illegal human-mutagen pipeline that transformed Dr. James Vance and Marcus Bell.
Lord Malachai: Dark-astral warlord who opened a sky-rift over Iron City to siphon Chris Thorne’s Ares bloodline before being banished into the void.
Overcharge & Gristle: Pierce’s hired enforcers—defeated in Issue #5 when Bryan overloaded Overcharge’s capacitors with alternating phase frequencies.
