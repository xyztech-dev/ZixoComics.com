Core Charter • Directive 01

THE PANTHEON-X PROTOCOL

Established atop Malachai's ruined spire at the close of Volume 1, the Pantheon-X Protocol is an off-grid mutual defense pact orchestrated by Bryan Cole (Electromark).

  • 01. Tactical Restraint: Raw power is never deployed without exit vectors and civilian containment calculated first.
  • 02. Zero Government Jurisdiction: Pantheon-X answers to no corporate board or black-site directorate.
  • 03. Humanity First: Every member retains a civilian anchor—ensuring gods and monsters never forget the streets below.
Author: Bryan Cole Status: Active

Corporate Black-Ops
